Lights Out: Reaction & Reflex

Motorsport-inspired reaction training with five game modes, global leaderboards, and 11 official team themes. Free on iOS, Android, and Apple TV.

Privacy Policy

Five Ways to Play

traffic

Launch Sequence

Five lights count down, then go dark. Tap the instant they go out. Millisecond-precision timing with F1 driver comparisons after every round.

grid_view

Reaction Grid

Tap highlighted cells on 3x3, 4x4, or 5x5 grids. Play untimed for accuracy or race the clock in 10-second timed mode.

tire_repair

Pit Lane

Two ways to play: chase a new high score every day in the Endurance challenge, or progress through the 275-level Circuit campaign across four racing eras. Catch the right tire color, build combos, and dodge distractors along the way.

group

Two-Player Split Screen

Face off on one device. Both players tap Ready, lights count down, and the fastest reaction wins. Instant rematch support.

view_in_ar

AR Mode (iOS only)

Place a 3D launch gantry in your room with augmented reality. Pinch to resize, tap to react. Full particle effects on iOS 18+.
